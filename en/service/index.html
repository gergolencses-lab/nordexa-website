Services

What is included in Nordexa lifecycle?

Not simply repairs — comprehensive lifecycle management, tailored to the machine type and the client's industry.

🔍

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled, periodic technical inspection: proactive replacement of wear parts, lubrication point servicing, calibration checks. Every intervention documented — audit-ready.

⚡

Emergency Response (SLA)

Guaranteed response time in case of machine stoppage — 4 h or 8 h depending on package. First a phone diagnosis, then an on-site engineer. No waiting around.

🔩

Spare Parts & Stock

Guaranteed stock of critical wear parts (seals, pump diaphragms, filling head inserts) at the client's plant or Nordexa's warehouse — with 24 h delivery.

📊

Predictive Diagnostics (IoT)

Built-in sensor system: vibration, temperature, pressure drop, operating hours monitoring. Automatic alert on anomaly — we intervene before a fault occurs.

🎓

Operator and Maintenance Training

Operator training at handover and refresher training as required. Your team should also be capable of first-level maintenance — this reduces service demand.

📋

Performance Reports and Audit Documentation

Annual OEE report, maintenance log and auditable documentation package — directly integrable into your quality management system.

How it works

The lifecycle contract process

01

Needs assessment and package selection

We discuss machine type, industry, uptime requirements and CEE accessibility. We determine the appropriate package.

02

Handover condition assessment

At handover or contract start our engineers record the machine condition — this is the basis for SLA calculation and guarantee.

03

Scheduled preventive maintenance

The annual maintenance calendar is aligned with your production schedule — we come in planned windows, not randomly.

04

Continuous monitoring and alerts (Uptime package)

IoT sensor system sends real-time data to Nordexa service centre — we raise an alert before a fault occurs.

05

Annual performance report and renewal

Year-end summary report: interventions carried out, parts replaced, OEE trend, recommendations for the following year. Contract renewal on transparent terms.
