The problem and the solution

When the standard catalogue is not enough

A custom dosing line is not a luxury — it is necessary when the product, formulation or manufacturing logic contains variables that an off-the-shelf product cannot handle.

Typical challenges

Constantly changing formulations and mixed batch sizes — the standard machine cannot keep up.

Unique material properties: high viscosity, reactive ingredients, light-sensitive or heat-sensitive products.

Confined plant space, specific hygiene requirements (GMP, ATEX), or the need to integrate with an existing line.

The previous supplier did not understand the plant logic — the machine exists but production is not optimal.

What Nordexa delivers

Engineering consultation from day one — not a template quote, but a real on-site needs assessment.

Modular internal architecture: the system is bespoke, but the modules are proven — lower risk, better serviceability.

Own manufacturing in Székesfehérvár — no subcontractor chain, quality stays in our hands.

Lifecycle guarantee: we do not disappear after handover — service, parts and expandability for decades.
