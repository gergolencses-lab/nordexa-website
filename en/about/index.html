Nordexa Industrial Solutions

Thirty years of building engineering solutions — where the standard catalogue is not enough.

We started in a Székesfehérvár workshop in 1994, with eleven people and a single client. Today we employ 480+ specialists, deliver to plants in six CEE countries, and the one thing that has not changed is our approach.

The story

From founding to the region's leading dosing machine manufacturer

In 1994, Gábor Horváth left the comfortable job at a socialist industrial company and founded a small machine assembly business in his wife's garage. The first machine was a packaging line — it took half a year, and was ordered by a local chemical company. They paid. That was Nordexa.

In the first decade we grew organically: not because we were aggressively market-driven, but because our clients spread the word through referrals. A good machine works for years — and the production manager who was satisfied with our work would, two years later at another plant, say "call Nordexa".

In 2003 we opened our first independent manufacturing hall in Székesfehérvár. By 2008 we had received ISO 9001 certification. By 2012 the export ratio had reached 40%. Today the six-country CEE client base accounts for more than half of orders — but our machines are still made where they were thirty years ago: in Székesfehérvár.

We are a mid-size company — intentionally. Large enough to deliver serious projects; small enough that the CEO's phone number is public and when you call, we pick up.

"I never wanted to build a big company. I wanted one where I would also be proud of what we ship."

— Gábor Horváth, founder and CEO

Our values

What has not changed in 30 years

Not slogans on a wall — these are the principles by which we actually measure our decisions.

🔧

Engineering integrity

We only promise what we can deliver. If a project is too complex for our capacity, we decline rather than deliver a half-result.

🕰

Long-term thinking

Every machine we design is built to work for 15–20 years. This costs more than the cheap option — but our clients return because they know it is true.

🤝

Partner, not supplier

We do not disappear after handover. The lifecycle contract is not just an offer — it is the way we take responsibility for what we have built.

🧩

The bespoke challenge is our comfort zone

Other manufacturers show a catalogue. We carry out a needs assessment. The best solution is the one that fits your plant — not the one easiest to manufacture.

🏡

Made in Hungary

Every machine is built in Székesfehérvár. No subcontractor chain, no outsourced assembly. Quality stays in our hands — and it shows in our references.

📐

Predictable implementation

Standard milestones, regular status reports, fixed deadlines. If we are running late, we tell you — not on delivery day.

Leadership

Facility

Székesfehérvár, Industrial Park — where the machines are born

Nordexa's entire manufacturing capacity is concentrated in Székesfehérvár. This is no accident: the city's industrial heritage, the engineering talent from proximity to Budapest University of Technology, and its logistics advantages (M7 motorway, 65 km from Budapest) offer the ideal location.

There is no external subcontractor chain in our factory — everything from component receipt to FAT testing is done by our own team. This may be slower than the outsourced model — but it is precisely why we can guarantee quality.
