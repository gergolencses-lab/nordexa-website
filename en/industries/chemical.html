What the chemical industry demands

Chemical dosing allows no compromise. Precision, safety and material compatibility are all required simultaneously — across changing materials and varying formulations.

🔥

ATEX zone management

In explosive atmospheres: fully spark-proof electrical systems with ATEX II 2G rating. Pneumatic drive option where electrical solutions pose a risk.

🧪

Corrosive and reactive materials

Acids, alkalis, solvents and reactive components — comprehensive material compatibility analysis with 316L stainless steel, PTFE, Hastelloy and other contact surfaces.

🌊

High viscosity

For materials above 500,000 cP: special filling head geometry, heated pipes and pumps. We ensure consistency where conventional systems clog or lose accuracy.

📋

REACH and safety documentation

Full IQ/OQ/PQ documentation with every system delivered, material compatibility certificates and REACH regulation compliance declaration.
