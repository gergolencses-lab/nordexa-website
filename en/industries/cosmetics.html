Challenges

What the cosmetics industry demands

In cosmetics filling, microbiological cleanliness, dosing accuracy and visual appearance all need to be right simultaneously. A bubble in a cream, a drop on the bottle, a gram off — each is a complaint.

🧼

Hygiene and microbiological cleanliness

Completely smooth internal surfaces, no dead zones — 316L stainless steel and PTFE contact parts, CIP/SIP-compatible design. Auditable cleaning process before and after every run.

🌡

Heat-sensitive emulsions

Serums and emulsions containing active ingredients are sensitive to heat and shear. Low-shear pump, temperature-controlled transport pipes and filling heads — so the product in the bottle is what was in the vessel.

💅

Premium packaging precision

In luxury cosmetics, drip-free filling and precise fill level are not optional — deviations are visible and immediately cause complaints. Anti-drip filling heads, fill level camera option.

🔄

Formulation and format changes

Seasonal, limited and premium lines in quick succession — the machine must switch in <15 minutes. Recipe library, automatic size-change filling heads, minimal manual intervention.
