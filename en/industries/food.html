Challenges

What the premium food industry demands

Food safety is non-negotiable — but the premium segment additionally demands formulation accuracy, fast changeovers and predictable, repeatable quality.

🛡

Food safety and HACCP

Every contact surface in food-safe material (316L stainless steel, PTFE, food-grade rubbers), with full HACCP-compatible documentation, CCP identification and validation.

⚠️

Allergen control and cross-contamination

When switching between allergen-containing and allergen-free products: auditable cleaning process, segregated material flows. CIP system validatable against the 14 EU allergens.

🌡

Heat-sensitive and particulate products

Premium oils, thick sauces, seed-containing mixes — particle integrity preserved with low-shear pump and specialist filling head geometry.

🔄

Seasonal and limited-edition runs

The premium food market is full of limited editions and seasonal products — the machine must change formulation in minutes, not days, with an auditable batch record.
